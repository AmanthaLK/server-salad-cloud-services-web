About Us

Server Salad Cloud Services is a Sri Lankan cloud hosting provider committed to delivering reliable, secure and customer-focused web hosting solutions. Since our founding in 2021, we have shaped every service around what our clients need, building our reputation on responsive support, transparency and lasting partnerships. We manage the infrastructure, security and day-to-day technical work, so our clients can focus on growing their businesses. Our in-house engineers provide 24/7 technical assistance, guiding every client from onboarding and website migration through daily operations. We operate with integrity, with transparent pricing, fully licensed software, no long-term lock-in and a firm commitment to sustainable, renewable-powered infrastructure. We are equally invested in Sri Lanka's digital future and offer dedicated programs for students, startups and independent professionals. Looking ahead, Server Salad is expanding into VPS hosting, dedicated servers, domain registration and business email, with the vision of becoming Sri Lanka's most trusted end-to-end cloud services partner. Based in Sri Lanka and serving clients worldwide, we remain focused on one goal: helping businesses grow online with confidence.
